## Activity 2 — Group-Chosen Dataset Analysis (4 points)

### Objective
Each group must select a dataset on a topic of their choice and apply the statistical methods studied in this course to analyze it and answer a self-proposed research question.

### Dataset Requirements
- Students are encouraged to use **real-world data** collected from experiments, surveys, or projects within their own major.
- Alternatively, groups may search for datasets from sources such as:
  - https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/datasets
  - https://www.kaggle.com/datasets
  - (or other equivalent sources)
- The dataset **must include**:
  - At least **2 categorical variables**
  - At least **4 numerical variables**

### Required Tasks

1. **Describe and clean the dataset** (if needed).
2. **Perform descriptive statistics** on the dataset.
3. **Apply appropriate models** (studied in this course) to answer the group's proposed research question(s):
   - Analyze and interpret the results.
   - Discuss, conclude, and propose recommendations (if any) based on the model findings.
